# dsh-project-mcp feature registry

> The single index of work. A row keeps its number for life; when a row outgrows the
> «File» column it points at a `docs/design/` document that carries the body — by default
> `docs/design/contracts/*` and the tests are enough, and they are what the «File» column
> names. The pre-F-21 wave history (`HANDOFF.md`) was deleted together with the document
> itself (with F-28): the live facts moved into the contracts, the rest is visible in git
> history.
>
> **Conventions:**
> - Statuses: `idea` → `backlog` → `in progress` → `done` / `cancelled`.
> - **Numbers are permanent.** Commits reference them (`F-NN` as the first word of the
>   summary). Renumbering and reuse are forbidden; a cancelled feature keeps its row with
>   the status `cancelled`.
> - Noticed a problem outside the current task? Add an `idea` row here and go back to
>   your work.
> - The large phased backlog is `docs/roadmap.md`; a taken unit gets its number here.
> - Definition of Done: `pnpm check` green (coverage ≥80), for client changes `pnpm e2e`
>   and `pnpm e2e --profile test-web` PASS, the tarball installed into the `web` profile,
>   the row updated, and a `feat:`/`fix:` commit plus a separate `release:` commit with
>   the feature number in the summary.
> - **F-33, F-35 and F-36 are claimed-but-never-registered.** The unapproved preview
>   proposal (now `docs/history/preview-proposal/`) assigned those numbers to itself, and
>   its F-34 collides with the registry's F-34 below. If the proposal is ever approved,
>   its units take fresh numbers — do not assign these three.
